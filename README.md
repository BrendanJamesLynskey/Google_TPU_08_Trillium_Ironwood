# Trillium &amp; Ironwood &mdash; TPU v6e and v7

Google's 2024&ndash;2025 chips. Trillium (v6e, May 2024 / GA Dec 2024) delivers a 4.7&times; jump on per-chip bf16 over v5e and 3rd-gen SparseCore. Ironwood (v7 / TPU7x, Apr 2025 / GA Nov 2025) ships 4.6 PFLOPS FP8 and 192 GiB HBM3e at 7.37 TB/s in a 9,216-chip pod &mdash; "first TPU for the age of inference". Per-chip and per-pod numbers in context vs NVIDIA H100/H200/B200.
